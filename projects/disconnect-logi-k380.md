# How to Disconnect the Logitech K380 Keyboard from a Mac or iPad

This guide will help you _disconnect and forget_ your Logi K380 Bluetooth keyboard from your Apple device. This process is called _unpairing_.

If you need to use your keyboard after, you will need to follow the [pairing procedure]({{ '/projects/logitech-k380.html' | relative_url }}) to connect your keyboard again.

### On your Mac or iPad

1. Go to **System Settings** (gear icon).
   
   ![Apple System Settings Icon](/assets/images/logi-keyb/apple-sys-settings.png)
   
2. Select the **Bluetooth** menu.
   
   ![Apple Bluetooth Menu](/assets/images/logi-keyb/bt-menu.png)
   
3. Make sure **Bluetooth** is on.
   
   ![Bluetooth Option On](/assets/images/logi-keyb/bt-on.png)
   
4. Press the ⓘ symbol on the right of **Keyboard K380** listed under **My Devices**.

   ![Circled "i" symbol next to keyboard on Devices list](/assets/images/logi-keyb/bt-my-keyb-dev.png)
   
5. Press the **Forget This Device…** button.
  
   ![Forget This Device button](/assets/images/logi-keyb/forget-keyb.png)
   
6. Choose **Forget Device** when the next warning is shown.

   ![Forget Bluetooth device warning](/assets/images/logi-keyb/forget-keyb-bt.png)
   
7. If your keyboard is currently turned on, the three indicator lights on top of the Bluetooth keys will flash briefly.

   ![Keyboard lights circled](/assets/images/logi-keyb/bt-keys-lights.jpg)
   
8. Your keyboard is now unpaired.
